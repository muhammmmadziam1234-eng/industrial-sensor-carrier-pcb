# Meridian Rev B first-article acceptance

Use a regulated 3.3 V supply with a current limit, a DMM, a scope, a known RS-485 peer, and a controller that can drive the carrier's 3.3 V interfaces. A differential probe or two matched probes referenced to board ground lets you measure A − B without grounding either bus wire. Use a short, common-ground bench bus. Record board and fixture IDs, hardware/firmware revisions, instruments, jumper settings, and ambient temperature before starting.

| ID | Bench setup and stimulus | First-article acceptance | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PWR-01 | Unpowered. Remove JP3 and disconnect the controller, auxiliary supply and peripherals. Check TP1 (rail), TP11 (controller input), TP12 (auxiliary input) to TP2 (ground), and check each input to TP1. | No hard short and no direct continuity from either source to TP1 with the shunt removed. Investigate a low reading that does not settle as capacitors charge. | Resistance readings after settling and photo of JP3. |
| PWR-02 | Set J7 supply to 3.30 V and start at a 50 mA current limit with all accessories disconnected. Fit JP3 2–3. After power-down repeat on J1 with JP3 1–2. Keep the inactive supply physically disconnected. | TP1 stays between 3.14 and 3.46 V once settled. Current limit does not remain active. Inactive source pin does not rise to the active rail. | Supply setpoint, TP1, both source-pin voltages, and steady-state current in both configurations. |
| BUS-01 | Unpowered, isolated from the peer. Measure A to B with JP1 open and then fitted. Connect the peer and terminate both cable ends before proceeding. | A–B resistance with JP1 fitted is consistent with a 120 Ω, 1% resistor and the transceiver input. With JP1 open, the isolated board reading is substantially higher. Confirm the fitted and removed states before powering. | Both A–B readings and a record of which ends are terminated. |
| BUS-02 | At 115,200 baud, exchange 1,000 known frames in each direction with sequence numbers and payload checks. Use an agreed reply delay and probe DIR, TX, and A − B. | Zero missing, duplicate or corrupt frames in either direction. DIR is low during reset, remains asserted through the last transmitted stop bit, and is low before the peer drives. Retain differential amplitude as a measurement under the stated load. | Frame totals, errors, peer/cable details, and scope captures with probe setup. |
| IO-01 | I²C at 100 kHz with the intended devices attached. Measure SDA and SCL from 30% to 70% of rail. Test SPI with a known device in the intended mode and clock range. | I²C rise time at or below 1.0 µs and no missing acknowledgements on 100 repeated reads. SPI identity/data bytes match and chip-select stays asserted for the intended transfer. | Jumper position, topology, speeds, rise times, and captured bytes. |
| ADC-01 | Drive a safe in-range step into each J5 input and probe the filtered TP8 or TP9 node. Repeat with the controller attached and sampling. | Record the 63% step time and compare with the nominal 100 µs from 1 kΩ × 100 nF. Set the firmware acquisition delay using the measured settling at the required accuracy. | Scope captures of both channels, sample timing, and observed settling. |
| OUT-01 | From a 3.3 V current-limited supply, use a resistive load sized for no more than 100 mA. Exercise one channel at a time, then both if the supply supports it. | Loads stay off on reset. Each commanded output sinks only its own load, and no unexpected heat or supply collapse occurs. | Gate/drain measurements, load currents, test duration and temperature change. |

The rail range, frame count, and test current are first-article bench criteria for this project. They are not component absolute maximums or a field rating. TI's THVD1410 data sheet specifies the differential output against particular loads and common-mode conditions. Keep the scope result and its termination setup together; do not compare an arbitrary bench trace to a differently loaded specification.

Release record: mark each row **not run**, **pass**, or **fail** and link its measurements. Stop on a sustained current-limit event, unexpected heating or backfeed. Record discrepancies before increasing speed, cable length or load. All rows are pending assembled hardware.
